# Code of Conduct

We want Kempt to be a welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers pledge to
treat everyone with respect, regardless of age, background, disability, identity,
experience, nationality, appearance, religion or orientation.

**Expected:** kind and constructive feedback, assuming good faith, accepting
disagreement gracefully, focusing on what is best for the project.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal or political
attacks, doxxing, sexualised language or attention, and other conduct that would
be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this, and
are responsible for applying it fairly. It applies in project spaces (issues,
PRs, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

Report problems privately through the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** form or by contacting the maintainers listed on the repository
profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
